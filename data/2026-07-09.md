### [1] [WildCity: A Real-World City-Scale Testbed for Rendering, Simulation, and Spatial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06838)
*Xiangyu Han,Mengyu Yang,Jiaqi Li,Bowen Chang,Ziyu Chen,Hexu Zhao,Rahul Kumar Agrawal,Anthony Rodriguez,Fiona Hua,Marco Pavone,Chen Feng,Yiming Li*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: WildCity是首个真实世界城市级别街景数据集与仿真平台，旨在填补大规模空间智能研究的数据空白，通过自治车队采集的连续多模态观测支持数字孪生重建与具身推理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有城市级3D重建研究受限于数据稀缺：合成数据存在sim-to-real鸿沟，真实驾驶数据集多为短片段且缺乏环绕视角连续覆盖，路线级数据在传感器覆盖与地理多样性上不足。
2. 基础模型在空间推理上面临小场景脆弱性：当前视觉语言模型在房间或街区尺度表现良好，但扩展到更大范围时难以保持空间一致性和长程依赖。
3. 缺乏仿真就绪的城市数字孪生环境：实现闭环仿真需要逼真的城市尺度重建，但现有方法在动态物体、光照变化、位姿噪声等真实挑战下鲁棒性不足。

Method: 1. 核心思路：通过真实城市街道的长时间自动驾驶采集，构建大规模多模态数据集，并提供从重建到仿真的完整研究框架。

3. 构建城市定制化重建基线：基于3D Gaussian Splatting设计适应长窄轨迹、稀疏重叠与传感器噪声的重建流程，将输出转换为支持闭环仿真的环境模型。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：WildCity首次提供城市尺度连续街景多模态数据与可运行仿真平台，为空间智能从实验室场景迈向真实世界规模提供了关键基础设施。

3. 关键takeaway：城市数字孪生的建设需要同时解决感知鲁棒性、可扩展架构与不确定性建模三大核心挑战，而不仅仅是提升重建质量。

To bridge the gap, we introduce WildCity, a real-world multimodal dataset collected by autonomous fleets traversing complex urban environments. Our dataset includes 18 trajectories, each averaging 83.7 kilometers in length, and preserves the core challenges of in-the-wild perception, e.g., dynamic objects, lighting variations, and imperfect camera poses. We further establish an urban-tailored reconstruction baseline and convert the reconstructed environments into a closed-loop simulator. Beyond the dataset and baseline, we systematically analyze the key challenges on the path to simulation-ready urban digital twins: scalability, extrapolation, and uncertainty. Ultimately, WildCity aims to catalyze progress not only in city-scale rendering, but more broadly in the pursuit of AI that can perceive, remember, and reason across space at a scale comparable to human cognition. ### [2] [NoDrift3R: Raymap-Guided Coupling for Drift-Robust Unposed Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07168)
*Xiangyu Sun,Liu Liu,Seungkwon Yang,Jingbing Han,Seungtae Nam,Zhizhong Su,Eunbyung Park*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: NoDrift3R在无姿态前馈3D重建中通过Raymap引导的几何-外观耦合模块和双频 viewpoints 调度，实现长序列下的鲁棒重建与姿态估计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有无姿态前馈3DGS在长序列中因累积姿态漂移导致几何建模错误与渲染质量显著下降。
2. 纯渲染监督在耦合优化几何与姿态时易陷入不稳定与局部最优；SfM提供的伪标签姿态又引入传感器噪声。
3. 现有方法的交互局限于低维姿态参数，信息瓶颈无法传递密集几何先验以约束高斯分布。

Method: 1. 核心思路：引入Raymap引导耦合，将高斯中心锚定到由raymap诱导的几何上，统一优化RGB重建、raymap一致性与相机正则化，形成渲染-几何双向增益循环。
2. RGC模块：用密集的raymap（像素级射线方向）替代低维姿态参数作为几何约束媒介，使外观与几何在像素层面共同引导高斯生成与优化。
3. 双频viewpoint调度：结合由易到难（高重叠到低重叠）的区间扩展与短间隔样本回放，稳定宽时间范围内的学习，抑制漂移。

Result: 在室内外跨域数据集（包括LLFF、Mip-NeRF 360、RealEstate10K等）上进行多场景、长序列实验，同时评估渲染质量与相机姿态估计精度，验证方法在长序列与宽基线下的鲁棒性与泛化能力。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：显式的几何-外观协同设计是无姿态前馈3D重建实现可扩展与抗漂移的关键；raymap作为稠密信息载体解除了低维姿态的信息瓶颈。
2. 主要限制：raymap质量与估计精度高度依赖训练数据的光照、纹理与运动覆盖；极端纹理缺失或快速非刚性运动场景下几何锚定失效，误差仍会累积。
3. 关键takeaway：双向反馈循环允许外观监督为几何提供强先验，同时几何约束稳定姿态优化，打破了传统两者耦合导致的局部最优陷阱。

Abstract: Pose-Free Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has recently emerged as a powerful paradigm for fast scene reconstruction. However, its performance degrades significantly in long image sequences due to cumulative camera pose estimation drift, which propagates errors into geometric modeling and severely limits rendering fidelity. In this work, we revisit the long-sequence bottleneck and identify pose drift as the primary factor restricting reconstruction quality. Furthermore, while SfM-based pseudo ground-truth poses introduce sensor noise, purely rendering-based supervision often leads to optimization instability and local minima due to the entangled optimization of geometry and pose. To address the challenges, we propose a synergistic pose-free framework that explicitly couples geometry and appearance via a Raymap-Guided Coupling Module (RGC). Concretely, we anchor Gaussian centers to raymap-induced geometry and jointly optimize RGB reconstruction, raymap consistency, and camera regularization under a unified objective, yielding a bidirectional feedback loop: stronger geometry improves rendering, and appearance supervision in turn refines geometry and pose. To further stabilize learning across wide temporal ranges, we introduce a Dual-Frequency Viewpoint Scheduling strategy that combines easy-to-hard interval expansion with replay of short-interval pairs. Extensive experiments across in-domain and cross-domain datasets show consistent gains in both rendering and pose estimation, with notably improved robustness on long sequences. Ablation studies validate our central insight: explicitly designed geometry-appearance synergy is the key to scalable and drift-robust pose-free feed-forward 3D reconstruction.

### [3] [PUF: Plug-and-Play Uncertainty-Aware Fusion for Online 3D Scene Graph Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07170)
*Yi Yang,Myrna Castillo,Bodo Rosenhahn,Michael Ying Yang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: PUF是一种用于在线3D场景图生成的即插即用框架，通过引入概率化融合机制，系统性地处理观测、2D模型和3D表示三方面不确定性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有在线3D场景图生成方法采用确定性融合流水线，未处理三方面不确定性：观测部分性导致的模糊、2D模型输出的软分布被硬化丢弃、3D表示因单帧深度噪声产生定位模糊。
2. 硬性二值关联与阈值规则导致证据无法在候选间有效传播，且难以完成稀疏共现关系的边构建。
3. 离线全局推理方法依赖完全重建，不适用于实时增量应用；在线方法虽快但缺乏不确定性建模，性能受限于确定性决策的误差累积。

Method: 1. 核心思路：将节点关联重构成联合语义与空间因素的似然问题，用狄利克雷证据累积在候选节点间按关联似然分配节点语义与关系证据，并通过类条件先验补全稀疏共现边。
2. 采用概率化节点关联：以类分布相似性与空间重叠构造似然，替代传统二值接受/拒绝门控，使几何不确定性可以弱化而非否决关联。
3. 引入狄利克雷证据累积：将2D模型输出的软类别与关系分布作为证据，随新观测按关联似然累加到对应节点的狄利克雷参数中，实现跨视图的分布级信息保持与传播。
4. 使用类条件先验完成边：针对从未或稀疏共现的对象对，利用先验关系分布为边赋予初始证据，解决在线场景中关系观测不足的问题。
5. 框架即插即用且无需训练：兼容不同3D表示后端（如高斯与体素），直接包装任意的软输出2D场景图模型，将不确定性从2D一直保留至3D融合阶段。

Result: 在3DSSG与ReplicaSSG两个基准上评估，PUF在保持实时延迟的情况下显著超越现有在线方法；同时在不增加训练与推理开销的前提下，在基于高斯与体素两种不同3D表示后端上均取得一致性能提升。

Conclusion: 1. 不确定性感知融合为在线3D场景理解提供了更稳健且可解释的构建路径，通过软概率融合有效缓解了确定性阈值的脆弱性问题。
2. 方法上限受限于2D模型的输出质量与不确定性校准程度；性能增益主要来源于更合理的证据传播而非更强的2D模型。
3. 当前框架假设观测独立且关联概率估计依赖简化几何模型，在动态遮挡与长时间尺度运动中可能累积系统误差；同时先验关系分布需从外部统计获得，对领域外泛化构成依赖。

Abstract: Online 3D scene graph generation builds a persistent, structured representation of a scene by incrementally fusing 2D observations into a global 3D graph. Existing online methods treat this fusion as a fully deterministic pipeline, where we identify three sources of uncertainty that are overlooked: observation, 2D model, and 3D representation. We propose PUF: a Plug-and-play, Uncertainty-aware, and training-free Fusion framework. Scene graph node association is reformulated as a probabilistic likelihood over semantic and spatial factors, replacing binary accept/reject gates. Dirichlet evidence accumulation distributes class and relationship evidence across plausible candidates proportional to association likelihood. An optional class-conditional prior completes edges for sparsely or never co-observed object pairs. We instantiate PUF with both a 3D Gaussian and a 3D voxel backend and observe consistent improvements, demonstrating its ability to generalize across different representations. Experiments on the 3DSSG and ReplicaSSG benchmarks show that our method substantially outperforms existing approaches while maintaining real-time latency. These results establish uncertainty-aware fusion as a principled and effective paradigm for online 3D scene understanding. The source code is publicly available at https://github.com/yyyyangyi/PUF.
