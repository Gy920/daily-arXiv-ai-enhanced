### [1] [Unpaired RGB-Thermal Gaussian-Splatting Using Visual Geometric Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05491)
*Jean Cordonnier,Chenghao Xu,Olga Fink,Malcolm Mielle*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出首个无配准RGB-热红外高斯泼溅（Gaussian Splatting）框架，通过跨模态位姿对齐与联合重建实现多模态新视角合成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有RGB-热红外三维重建依赖精确配准图像对或立体配置，难以规模化部署。
2. 既有方法未考虑无配准场景下跨模态几何一致性问题，仅以单模态指标评估导致重建结果在模态间出现空间错位。

Method: 1. 核心思路：使用VGGT独立估计RGB与热红外图像的相机位姿，通过跨模态特征匹配（XoFTR）与Procrustes变换实现位姿集对齐，进而进行多模态联合高斯泼溅重建。
2. 利用VGGT分别对RGB与热红外图像序列进行前馈式位姿估计，无需跨模态匹配。
3. 设计跨模态特征匹配器（XoFTR）提取对应点，并使用Procrustes算法求解刚体变换，将热红外位姿对齐至RGB参考系。
4. 定义跨模态与模态内评估指标，以量化重建结果的模态一致性与各模态自身质量，解决无配准场景下评价标准缺失的问题。

Result: 在ThermalGaussian与ThermoScenes的九个场景上进行多场景验证，覆盖室内外多样化条件；与配对数据的ThermalGaussian基准对比，本方法在新视角合成质量与交叉模态对齐度上接近配对条件下限，但位姿估计误差仍导致跨模态轻微不对准；所提评估框架揭示仅依赖单模态指标会掩盖模态间不一致问题。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：首次实现无需配准的RGB-热红外联合高斯泼溅重建，并通过跨模态位姿对齐与双重评估指标解决多模态一致性问题。
2. 主要限制：位姿对齐依赖XoFTR的跨模态特征匹配稳定性，且VGGT未保证重建场景的绝对尺度，导致最终几何对齐存在残余误差；实验仅涵盖VGGT+XoFTR单一路径，未探索其他特征提取器。
3. 关键takeaway：多模态三维重建必须同时评估模态内质量与跨模态一致性，否则独立优化的单模态重建会被误认为成功联合重建。

Abstract: Multi-modal novel view synthesis (NVS) combining RGB and thermal imagery enables precise 3D scene reconstruction with visual and thermal information. However, existing methods typically rely on precisely calibrated RGB-thermal image pairs or stereo setups, limiting scalability and practical deployment. To address this, we introduce a framework for unpaired RGB-thermal NVS that leverages VGGT, a 3D feed-forward transformer architecture, to independently estimate camera poses for each modality. The pose sets are then aligned using the Procrustes algorithm with a cross-modal feature matcher, enabling joint registration without paired calibration. Building on this alignment, we further propose a multi-modal 3D Gaussian Splatting approach that learns directly from unpaired RGB and thermal images. Experiments on diverse scenes demonstrate that our method achieves competitive performance in thermal view synthesis while maintaining RGB fidelity. Moreover, we show that existing reconstruction approaches can produce modality-specific reconstructions that lack cross-modal consistency. We thus introduce a benchmarking framework to rigorously evaluate both per-modality image synthesis and the multi-modal coherence of reconstructed scenes.

### [2] [Let It Be Simple: One-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05737)
*Yitong Chen,Shiduo Zhang,Jingjing Gong,Xipeng Qiu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文指出视觉语言动作模型中动作生成的条件-目标结构与图像生成不同，提出仅通过偏置训练时间分布到高噪声状态即可实现强单步生成，无需复杂的少步扩散方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有扩散VLA模型沿袭图像生成的多步迭代范式，但VLA的条件（多模态观测）与目标（低维动作块）存在不对称性，导致少步生成未必需要图像合成中的复杂蒸馏或一致性训练。
2. 动作生成任务的本质是紧凑目标预测，而图像生成需要处理高维多模态目标，这导致直接套用少步图像生成技术可能带来不必要的开销。

Method: 1. 核心思路：在标准的流匹配目标下，将训练时的时间采样分布向高噪声状态偏置，使模型在训练中更多地学习从噪声到干净动作的映射，从而提升单步推理质量。
2. 保持标准速度预测头与VLM编码器架构，不引入教师模型、蒸馏阶段或辅助目标，仅修改时间分布，实验在控制性MNIST网格到序列任务与真实机器人VLA任务上验证。
3. 关键新意：将VLA的单步动作生成问题视为条件-目标结构问题，并以轻量级修改（时间分布偏置）实现了无需蒸馏的强单步性能，挑战了必须使用复杂少步扩散工具的观点。

Result: 在LIBERO、LIBERO-Plus、LIBERO-Pro标准套件以及真实双手机器人YAM RSS挑战中，使用高噪声偏置训练时间分布的一步策略通常与十步解码性能相当，在标准LIBERO上甚至优于十步均匀分布训练策略；在1.4B VLM加30M动作头的配置下，一步解码在LIBERO-Long上达到95.6%成功率。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：证明了强单步VLA动作生成可由标准扩散训练自然涌现，无需引入为图像生成设计的完整少步扩散框架，从而在保持性能的前提下大幅降低推理开销。
2. 深层限制：方法有效性依赖于“条件信息充分、动作目标低维且相对简单”的前提，当条件或任务结构改变时，高噪声偏置训练可能失效；且性能提升幅度受限于VLM编码器对场景与任务的表示质量。
3. 关键认知：VLA的单步生成优势根植于其不对称的条件-目标结构，时间分布偏置作为简单正则化手段能有效校准速度场，使单步预测更贴近条件均值的低方差区域。

Abstract: Diffusion-based vision-language-action (VLA) models often inherit the image-generation view: actions are generated by iterative denoising. We argue that VLA action generation has a different condition-target structure: the policy is conditioned on rich observations, language, and state, but predicts only a compact, low-dimensional action chunk. Under this asymmetry, strong one-step action generation should not necessarily require the advanced one-step methods developed for image synthesis. We keep standard velocity prediction and add no teacher model, distillation stage, or auxiliary objective; in our main recipe, we simply bias the training time distribution toward high-noise states. We first isolate the effect in a controlled MNIST grid-to-sequence task, then test it with extensive robot-policy experiments. Across standard LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, and LIBERO-Pro, one-step policies trained with high-noise biased schedules generally match ten-step decoding under the same recipe, and on standard LIBERO can exceed ten-step policies trained with a uniform time distribution. A real-robot bimanual YAM RSS evaluation gives a small-sample cross-architecture check of the same sampler trend. On a 1.4B VLM model with a 30M action head, one-step decoding reaches 95.6\% on LIBERO-Long. These results show that strong one-step VLA action generation can emerge from standard diffusion training, without importing the full few-step diffusion machinery developed for image generation.

### [3] [HDST-GNN: Heterogeneous Dynamic Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks for Multi-Object Tracking in UAV Aerial Imagery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05587)
*Phillip Jiang*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 将图神经网络框架扩展至无人机多目标追踪场景，通过异质图建模、高度自适应连边和遮挡门控注意力解决无人机视角下的尺度变化、状态异质性和频繁遮挡问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 固定空间半径的图连边方式无法适应无人机在不同高度下的尺度变化
2. 同质图建模忽略检测、活跃轨迹和丢失轨迹的异构语义
3. 未区分节点遮挡状态，导致遮挡节点错误影响邻居嵌入传播
4. 现有图神经网络方法在无人机小目标密集场景下性能受限

Method: 1. 核心：构建异质动态时空图，显式建模三类节点与五类边关系，并提出高度自适应连边与遮挡门控注意力机制
2. 高度自适应连边：根据帧内平均目标面积估计相对高度代理，动态缩放图连接半径
3. 异质节点与边表示：对检测、活跃轨迹、丢失轨迹节点使用独立投影，定义五种带类型偏置的有向边关系
4. 遮挡门控时空聚合：在注意力计算后引入基于遮挡置信度的标量门，缩放消息传递强度
5. 端到端训练：采用可微Sinkhorn匹配头与联合交叉熵与三元组损失

Result: 在VisDrone2019-MOT数据集上，使用真值检测与YOLOv8n检测两种设置进行验证，相比基线显著降低ID切换次数并提升IDF1与MOTA，消融实验表明各模块独立贡献正向

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：首次将异质图神经网络引入无人机多目标追踪，通过高度自适应与遮挡门控有效应对尺度变化、状态异构与遮挡
2. 主要限制：高度代理仅基于平均面积可能受前景背景杂乱分布影响；可微Sinkhorn分配复杂度高，实时性受限；使用ResNet-18的静态外观特征提取器在小目标上鉴别力有限
3. 关键takeaway：无人机追踪需要图结构的动态与异构设计，节点的生命周期状态与空间上下文应解耦建模

Abstract: Multi-object tracking (MOT) from UAV imagery presents unique challenges: altitude varies across sequences, objects are small and densely packed, and frequent occlusion causes identity switches. Existing graph-based trackers assume fixed spatial context and treat all objects uniformly, ignoring the heterogeneous lifecycle states of detections, active tracklets, and lost targets. We propose HDST-GNN, a Heterogeneous Dynamic Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Network with three novel contributions. First, Altitude-Adaptive Edge Construction estimates a camera-altitude proxy from mean object area and adjusts the graph connectivity radius accordingly. Second, Heterogeneous Node Representation models detections (Type-D), confirmed tracklets (Type-T), and lost tracklets (Type-L) as distinct node types with dedicated projections and typed edge relations. Third, Occlusion-Gated Temporal Aggregation gates each node's attention contribution by its occlusion confidence, preventing occluded nodes from corrupting neighbour embeddings. HDST-GNN is trained end-to-end with a differentiable Sinkhorn head using joint cross-entropy and triplet loss. On VisDrone2019-MOT with oracle detections, HDST-GNN achieves 94.51% MOTA and 97.24% IDF1, outperforming SORT by +5.0 MOTA points and reducing identity switches by 81%. With real YOLOv8n detections, HDST-GNN reduces identity switches by 49% vs. SORT. Ablation studies confirm the independent contribution of each component.

The project page is available at https://github.com/TanmouTT/DisasterBench.

### [5] [PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05773)
*Chong Ma,Taiyi Su,Jian Zhu,Jianjun Zhang,Zitai Huang,Yi Xu,Hanli Wang*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 针对 VLA 策略闭环评估缺口，提出了分段式世界模型 PiL-World，通过将策略预测的动作块转化为视觉控制条件，并结合多视角历史记忆与联合预测，实现与真实闭环执行高度一致的想象轨迹，显著降低成功率估计误差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有世界模型多限于开环预测，无法支持 VLA 策略的闭环评估接口。
2. 简单估计任务级成功不够，还需要过程中无幻觉、多视角一致的端到端轨迹一致性。

Method: 1. 核心思路：以块为单位，将 VLA 输出的动作块投影为视觉控制条件，并加入潜在多视角历史记忆，联合预测下一段多帧多视角观测，使生成的观测可反馈给策略以形成闭环。
2. 动作块→视觉控制：将绝对关节空间动作块通过投影映射为头部视角的时序运动视频，作为视觉先验。
3. 潜在历史记忆：将近期观测（真实初始+后续生成）编码为时序潜在记忆，提供任务执行上下文以保持延续性。
4. 联合多视角生成：显式同步预测所有相机视角的未来帧，保障跨视角时空一致性。
5. 训练数据策略：除成功演示外，还利用失败的遥操作轨迹，使想象分布更贴近真实策略执行分布。

Result: 在三个真实双臂操作任务（分类方块、堆叠碗、堆叠木块）上验证。以真实闭环执行为基准，包络式评估显示：想象轨迹的最终成功率与真实执行更接近，误差降至约 12%；提出幻觉停止率（HFR）度量生成长程可靠性；单步多视角感知相似性指标显示动作条件生成质量良好。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：首次将世界模型嵌入 VLA 闭环评估接口，提供可扩展、低成本的策略测试床，误差显著低于先前方法。
2. 深层限制：依赖稳定的头部相机视野；潜在历史记忆容量与时间范围有限，对长程依赖可能衰减；多任务泛化仍需验证超出预训练分布的任务。
3. 关键 takeaway：将动作投影为视觉控制与联合多视角生成是维持闭环一致性的关键；纳入失败样本对捕捉真实策略多样性至关重要。

Abstract: Vision-language-action (VLA) policies operate in a closed loop in real-world robot tasks: a robot observes the scene, executes an action chunk, and conditions its next decision on the resulting observation. However, most existing world models for robot action evaluation are limited to open-loop prediction along pre-collected action trajectories. This prevents them from supporting closed-loop VLA evaluation, where each action chunk must be conditioned on the observation generated by the previous execution. To address this gap, we propose PiL-World, a chunk-wise world model designed for policy-in-the-loop VLA evaluation. Given the current observation and the action trajectory rolled out by a VLA policy, PiL-World generates multi-view future observations that are consistent with the VLA rollout and match the image inputs required by the policy. By alternating between VLA inference and world-model prediction, PiL-World enables closed-loop evaluation without real robot execution at every step. To improve rollout fidelity, PiL-World conditions video generation on action-derived visual control from head-view robot motion and latent histories that encode task execution context, while jointly predicting complementary multi-view observations. Beyond successful teleoperated demonstrations, it also learns from failed execution trajectories, helping the imagined rollouts better match the distribution of real policy executions. We evaluate PiL-World on three real dual-arm manipulation tasks. PiL-World generates imagined rollouts that are highly consistent with real robot executions. More importantly, compared with the baseline, it reduces the error between VLA success rates measured in real-world rollouts and those estimated through closed-loop world-model evaluation from 63.2% to 12.0%.
