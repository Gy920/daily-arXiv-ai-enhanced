### [4] [FBFM: A Training-Free Asynchronous Feedback Mechanism for Flow-Matching in World-Action Models Execution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29235)
*Peize Li,Ruimeng Zhang,Ru Zhang,Cong Huang,Kai Chen,Shanghang Zhang*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: FBFM提出将chunk级反馈下推到flow matching的单个solver evaluation步，通过masked pseudoinverse correction实现跨chunk异步闭环纠错，以无训练方式弥合WAM开环生成与真实世界动态之间的粒度鸿沟。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有WAM的chunk级重锚定（re-grounding）时间粒度不足，无法纠正正在生成chunk内的单步预测偏差
2. Chunk间直接用真值刷新历史/KV cache仅提供生成条件，而非对当前chunk中特定时间步变量的硬约束
3. 长程任务中未校正的预测漂移会导致动作基于机器人永远无法到达的未来状态

Method: 1. 在flow matching推理过程中，将前序动作执行后的实测状态与后续chunk的时间对齐变量进行masked pseudoinverse投影，形成异步反馈闭环
2. 动态部分观测生成框架：将环境执行窗口揭示的实时观测作为部分边界条件，锚定到正在生成的chunk的特定时间槽，而非仅作为后续生成的上下文
3. Masked pseudoinverse速度修正：对条件速度场施加伪逆投影，使受观测约束的坐标收敛到测量值，同时保持未观测坐标遵循预训练先验
4. 跨模态/跨阶段统一接口：stage-wise与joint-generation模型均通过clean-endpoint Jacobian的跨模态块将状态残差直接传播到动作坐标，无需重新训练
5. 异步时序解耦：前序chunk执行期间即可完成对后续chunk的修正，打破传统chunk边界处才能反馈的时序耦合

Result: 在RoboTwin2.0与LIBERO基准上验证了两种WAM架构（DreamZero joint-generation、LingBot-VA stage-wise）；RoboTwin2.0多个任务配置成功率获得显著提升，LIBERO部分suite有改善；真实机器人观测序列诊断显示状态跟踪精度提升。所有改进均无需重新训练WAM主干网络。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：证明无训练推理干预可在不破坏预训练生成先验的前提下实现细粒度在线纠错，将WAM从开环预测范式推进到异步闭环执行
2. 主要限制：增益高度依赖观测延迟与任务动态的匹配程度；masked pseudoinverse在不同flow matching参数化下的数值稳定性未充分讨论；极低延迟场景下异步执行窗口可能不足
3. 关键takeaway：重锚定的本质是将环境反馈作为生成过程中的硬约束而非软条件，FBFM为此提供了与WAM架构无关的通用flow matching推理接口

Abstract: Although world-action models (WAMs) enhance long-horizon robot control by predicting visual evolution before acting, long-horizon reliability demands repeated re-grounding in real observations--not recursive rollout. Existing WAMs address this by refreshing history or KV cache with ground-truth data between chunks. However, such chunk-wise feedback operates at a coarse temporal granularity and thus fails to correct prediction errors at the individual time-step level. To address this, we propose Feedback Flow Matching (FBFM), a training-free inference mechanism that pushes re-grounding inside the actively generated chunk. During flow matching, FBFM applies a masked pseudoinverse correction to the conditional velocity field: it leverages the preceding action chunk to guide generation of the next action chunk, and uses the image observed after executing that preceding chunk to guide the next frame prediction. This cross-chunk pairing--where feedback from one chunk arrives in time to shape the next--creates an asynchronous loop that corrects errors without waiting for chunk boundaries. Being training-free, the mechanism improves responsiveness to unexpected events and suppresses drift in long-horizon tasks. We evaluate FBFM on both a joint-generation WAM (DreamZero) and a stage-wise WAM (LingBot-VA). On selected LIBERO and RoboTwin2.0 tasks, it improves success rates by over 5% in favorable settings, and real-world robot observation-prediction diagnostics show notably better tracking. We argue that FBFM offers a new paradigm for fine-grained online correction, bridging open-loop flow generation with closed-loop real-world dynamics.

Because retrieved skills shape on-policy training trajectories, their benefits can enter the model parameters as well as remain in the external bank, enabling memory-free deployment and optional inference-time retrieval. Across seven open-domain and multi-hop question-answering benchmarks, SESA improves average accuracy over SSP by 1.2--3.2 points across multiple backbones and surpasses the skill-augmented SkillRL baseline by 0.9 points under a unified evaluation protocol. On Qwen3 models, SESA-Off retains 1.8--2.2 points of improvement over SSP, while the final skill bank adds a further 0.5--1.0 points. These results show that evolving skill memory is not merely an inference-time plug-in: it changes policy learning and the future training distribution while retaining value as optional external memory.
