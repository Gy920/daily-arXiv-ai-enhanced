### [1] [TSCA-Net: Temporal-Spatial Clique Attention for Interpretable Multimodal Pedestrian Trajectory Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11939)

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 目标条件化模型依赖静态位移结构，对所有历史时间步赋予相同权重，无法实现时间感知的目标-历史交互。

Comprehensive ablation studies confirm the complementary contributions of all three proposed modules.
