### [1] [From Hallucination to Grounding: Diagnosing Visual Spatial Intelligence via CRISP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26535)
*Zhixing Li,Yinan Yu*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: CRISP 是一种新型结构性诊断评估范式，通过跨任务一致性（语言推理与3D场景图生成的对齐）来解耦视觉空间智能中的感知与推理瓶颈，揭示现有VLM存在系统性的感知-推理脱节。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 传统VQA评估易被语言先验污染，无法区分真实空间推理与语义捷径。
2. 现有3D空间探测（如认知地图、文本解释）存在幻觉或缺乏度量精度。
3. 当前评估缺乏对模型内部隐式3D结构表示的显式验证机制。
4. 需从结果导向的正确性转向过程导向的感知-推理一致性诊断。

Method: 1. 核心思路：将空间问答（Spatial QA）与3D场景图构建（SGC）耦合，以生成的结构性表示作为信息瓶颈强迫模型显式化隐式空间建模。
2. 设计跨任务一致性协议：比较QA答案的几何合理性与SGC的结构拓扑，以验证推理是否真正被视觉所支撑。
3. 提出干预诊断实验：对比在真实结构指导与预测图下的表现，解耦感知瓶颈与推理能力。
4. 构建CRISP基准：从nuScenes和ScanNet++自动滤除遮挡/低质样本，生成带唯一数值ID的主3D场景图，避免语义泄露。
5. 关键新意：以度量级3D结构为锚点，将抽象空间直觉转化为可验证的几何坐标，超越传统黑盒QA的模糊评估。

Result: 在1,162个室内外静态场景、9,839个问题上的多任务验证表明：专有模型虽具备强推理引擎但度量估计不准且未利用隐式结构；开源模型则受限于多跳组合推理。实验覆盖距离/尺寸/方向/计数/排序/视角变换/逻辑演绎七类空间能力，并使用真实传感器数据构建真值场景图，但未涉及动态视频序列。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：揭示当前VLM存在系统性的感知-推理脱节，指出结构化对齐比提升逻辑能力更紧迫。
2. 主要限制：场景选择偏静态且高度过滤，可能无法代表极端遮挡或复杂动态环境；跨任务一致性依赖SGC的生成质量，模型若全部失败则无法诊断。
3. 关键takeaway：未来实现具身智能应优先建立精确的度量级场景结构表示，而非仅优化端到端推理能力。

Abstract: Current VLM evaluations often conflate language priors with genuine spatial reasoning. To address this, we introduce CRISP, a novel structural-diagnostic evaluation paradigm that assesses visual spatial intelligence through consistency, the alignment between implicit perception and explicit reasoning. Unlike traditional black-box QA, CRISP utilizes metric 3D Scene Graphs and an oracle intervention protocol to decouple latent reasoning capabilities from perceptual bottlenecks. This granular diagnosis uncovers a systematic perception-reasoning disconnect. Crucially, we reveal that while proprietary models possess robust latent reasoning engines, they suffer from inaccurate metric estimation and a critical failure to leverage their implicit structural representations. Conversely, open-source models remain fundamentally bottlenecked by their lack of multi-hop compositional reasoning. By shifting the focus from merely ``guessing correctly'' via language priors to genuinely ``perceiving, verifying, and reasoning,'' CRISP offers a rigorous roadmap for multimodal alignment beyond end-to-end post-training. 5. 设计可扩展的数据加载系统（abcdl）与推理优化，支持分布式训练和云存储加载，以处理大规模多流视频数据。

Result: 验证覆盖离线指标（loss、动作误差）、模拟性能和真实机器人执行，包括长时域与灵巧任务（如连续插入6个AirPods、折叠纸盒、从钱包中取出信用卡），均成功演示。模拟与真实评估结果具相关性，使无实体机器人者能进行设计迭代。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：通过完全开源的堆栈（数据、模型、代码、评估）建立行为克隆研究的共同基线，推动领域透明与快速进展。
2. 主要限制：数据集集中于单一低成本双手机器人平台（Agibot G1），其泛化至其他构型或更大规模系统仍需验证；模拟与真实的相关性虽被展示，但系统性能上限仍受真实数据规模与质量制约。
3. 关键takeaway：BC的规模化需要系统性工程投入（数据格式、加载、训练系统、模拟对齐），单点突破不足以支撑可复现的研究生态。

Abstract: We introduce ABC, a fully open-source stack for manipulation with behavior cloning. At its core is ABC-130K: the largest open-source teleoperation dataset to date, featuring 3,500 hours of data spanning over 130K episodes across 195 diverse tasks. Furthermore, we open-source our accessible hardware setup, training infrastructure, and simulation pipeline. We also release 400 hours of sim-teleop data and provide a co-training recipe that produces correlated simulation and real-world evaluation, offering a reliable proxy for ablating model-design and training decisions before costly real-world evaluation. We explore various training recipes and compare common architectural choices for Diffusion Transformers (DiT) and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, grounding our findings in real-world evaluations. The resulting policies successfully execute dexterous tasks such as box folding and extracting credit cards from wallets. By providing a reproducible toolkit, we aim to place researchers on an equal footing, establishing the necessary foundation to learn the ABCs of Behavior Cloning together as a community.
