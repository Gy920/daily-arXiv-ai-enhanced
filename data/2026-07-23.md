### [1] [Extending a Large View Synthesis Model for Multi-view Panoptic Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19765)
*Kwonyoung Ryu,In-Jae Lee,Jonghyun Jin,Hyunjee Lee,Jongmin Lee,Jaesik Park*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文首次将大规模视图合成模型从外观渲染扩展到3D场景理解，证明无需显式3D重建和任务特定训练即可实现多视角全景分割。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有全景分割方法依赖显式3D重建（如NeRF或3DGS），重建误差直接传递至分割结果

Method: 1. 核心思路：利用仅用RGB训练的大规模视图合成模型的内在跨视角注意力机制，将全景标签从输入视角传播到新视角，无需修改模型参数或进行分割特定训练。
2. 编码方案：将全景标签转换为二进制通道表示，与RGB输入使用相同的token化流程。
3. 架构设计：采用双路径设计，RGB重建路径保持冻结，分割路径共享相同的交叉注意力渲染器，实现解耦的可替换模块。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：证明视图合成模型学习的空间对应关系是输入无关的几何对应，为无需3D重建的3D理解任务开辟新途径。
2. 主要限制：方法依赖于源视图已有高质量全景标签，且对输入标签的跨视角一致性极为敏感；模型未显式建模遮挡关系，可能导致未观察区域的标记传播出现噪声。
3. 关键takeaway：冻结的RGB渲染模型可作为通用空间对应函数，为视图不变信号提供高质量传播，实现外观渲染与语义理解的解耦设计。

These results indicate that the correspondence learned for RGB view synthesis can also propagate view-independent per-pixel labels. Abstract: Existing teleoperation systems are often tailored to specific robot hardware and task domains, limiting their scalability and adaptability. We present ModPack, a modular and extensible teleoperation system designed to support diverse robot embodiments and task requirements within a unified framework. At the core of ModPack is a self-contained wearable "backpack" that integrates onboard computation, power, communication, and data storage. Built on top of this shared interface, the system supports plug-and-play capability modules including joint-level teleoperation with haptic feedback, mobile manipulation, and active perception. Experiments across two distinct robot platforms and real-world mobile manipulation tasks demonstrate that ModPack provides a flexible and reusable framework for data collection and policy learning. To support future research, we open-source the complete hardware design and software stack. Project website: https://modpack-robotics.github.io/
