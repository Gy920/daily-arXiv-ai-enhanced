### [1] [HumanNet: Scaling Human-centric Video Learning to One Million Hours](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06747)
*Yufan Deng,Daquan Zhou*

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 1. 核心思路：将互联网异构视频系统化转化为具身预训练就绪的基础设施，强调规模、视角多样性和物理相关性

### [2] [AdpSplit: Error-Driven Adaptive Splitting for Faster Geometry Discovery in 3D Gaussian Splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06876)
*Yongjae Lee,Jingxing Li,Abhay Kumar Yadav,Rama Chellappa,Deliang Fan*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: AdpSplit 通过误差驱动的自适应分裂机制，替代 3DGS 中固定Cardinality的随机分裂，在更少的densification迭代中实现同等渲染质量，从而加速训练。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 标准 3DGS 的 ADC 采用固定Cardinality的随机分裂（二元分裂），导致在短训练周期内无法快速充足化场景结构，成为加速瓶颈。
2. 单纯增加分裂子体数量（如5个）并不能改善质量，因为随机初始化导致冗余、噪声或错误覆盖，与误差分布解耦。

Method: 1. 核心：以视图误差为驱动，自适应确定分裂子体数量与参数，替代固定随机分裂。
2. 误差区域划分：对每个分裂候选高斯，渲染采样视图，识别高误差像素并归属到该高斯；将像素划分为连通误差区域，每个区域提出一个子体。
3. 子体初始化：从误差区域统计反投影估计位置（深度对齐父高斯），协方差由区域分布估计，颜色直接来自观测值。
4. 跨视图合并：不同视图对同一高斯分裂可能产生重叠子体，合并冗余提案以避免重复添加相同结构。

Result: 在 MipNeRF360、Deep-Blending、Tanks&Temples 等多个标准数据集上，作为即插即用替换，AdpSplit 将多种加速 3DGS 流水线的训练时间减少 9.2%-22.3%；与 FastGS 结合可在 MipNeRF360 上匹配全周期 PSNR 同时减少训练时间 16.4%，相对 vanilla 3DGS 加速 12.6 倍。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：将分裂操作与局部误差结构绑定，显著降低densification所需迭代，打破固定Cardinality瓶颈。
2. 主要限制：额外计算开销在于误差渲染、像素聚类与跨视图合并，可能影响每densification步骤的时长；误差区域统计依赖采样视图密度与均匀性。
3. 关键洞察：分割有效性关键在于误差归属与区域统计的稳定性，而非单纯增加子体数量；质量提升主要来自误差驱动的参数初始化而非Cardinality变化。

Abstract: Adaptive density control in 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) repeatedly grows the Gaussian population through fixed-cardinality random splitting to discover useful scene structure. However, in vanilla 3DGS, its binary split operator requires many densification rounds to expose fine details, making it a bottleneck for efficient training schedules with fewer iterations. We introduce AdpSplit, an error-driven adaptive split operator that determines the number of split children and initializes the child parameters from L1-pixel-error region statistics, enabling fewer densification iterations, thus reduced training time, while preserving the rendering quality of full-schedule training. Across the MipNeRF360, Deep-Blending, and Tanks&Temples datasets, AdpSplit reduces the training time of multiple accelerated 3DGS pipelines by 9.2%-22.3% as a simple drop-in replacement for the standard split operator. 3. 关键takeaway：隐式动作的低维流匹配预测比高维特征直接回归/生成更可行；将世界模型作为“特征预测器+低维生成接口”是效率与泛化的关键权衡。

Visual feature-based world models, on the other hand, predict future visual features instead of raw video pixels, offering a promising alternative that is more efficient and less prone to hallucination. The second one is the first visual RL framework trained entirely inside a world model learned from offline videos only, using a video-aligned reward and no online interactions or handcrafted rewards.
