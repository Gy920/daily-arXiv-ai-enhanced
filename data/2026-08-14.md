### [1] [Can Vision-Language Models Assess Proxemic Risk from Egocentric Robot Images?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12515)
*Vladyslava Rudas,Dmytro Kuzmenko*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文针对机器人第一人称视角下的近身风险评估任务，系统性评估了三类开源VLM的细粒度推理与空间定位能力，构建了首个基于JRDB的该场景标注数据集，验证了提示工程与轻量化微调的效果边界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有机器人近身风险评估多依赖几何推理或专用深度传感器，缺乏对通用VLM在该任务上能力的系统性验证。
2. 当前VLM的空间推理局限已被广泛指出，但从未从机器人共享人机环境的第一人称视角评估其近身风险分类与空间定位的一致性。
3. VLM的提示策略、轻量化微调对近身风险这类细粒度场景理解的效果尚未得到实证研究。

4. 额外分析分类正确性与人物空间定位能力的关联，验证模型风险标签输出是否依赖对目标场景区域的关注。

3. 关键takeaway：通用VLM目前尚不能可靠替代传统几何/深度传感器的近身风险评估方案，仅能作为特定场景下的高风险检测辅助补充。

However, \textit{Qwen-VL} with an advanced prompt achieves substantially higher recall for high-danger cases than the other models. An analysis of person localization further shows that correct danger classification does not correspond to better spatial grounding, indicating that a model may produce a useful safety label without attending to the relevant region of the scene. These results show that current VLMs remain limited in fine-grained proxemic reasoning and spatial grounding, although targeted prompting and fine-tuning can improve high-danger detection in selected models.

### [2] [LocusGS: Spatially Grounded Tokens for Feed-Forward 3D Gaussian Splatting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12825)
*Wenyu Li,Sidun Liu,Tongrui Hu,Peng Qiao,Yong Dou*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 针对前馈式3DGS中查询token空间相干性不足的问题，LocusGS通过引入显式3D锚点状态（中心+支撑半径）为每个查询提供空间定位能力，在保持固定高斯预算的同时实现更结构化的场景表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有查询式前馈3DGS方法将查询表示为纯潜空间令牌，缺乏显式空间状态，导致同一查询解码的高斯散布在场景遥远区域，空间相干性弱。
2. 纯潜空间查询无法在多视图特征聚合和高斯解码阶段提供显式空间约束，造成query-wise空间组织与场景结构对齐差。

Method: 1. 核心思路：为每个高斯查询配备可逐步细化的显式3D锚点状态（中心+支撑半径），将空间 grounding 注入整个解码过程。
2. 锚点状态设计：每个查询包含特征嵌入和3D锚点（中心+支撑半径），锚点作为空间参考指导后续所有计算。
3. 几何引导的特征聚合：通过锚点到射线的几何偏置（anchor-to-ray geometric bias）引导交叉注意力，使查询聚合与当前锚点位置空间相关的多视图观测。
4. 局部高斯解码：以锚点为中心解码高斯，将其组织在锚点定义的局部支撑区域内，确保同一查询生成的组内高斯保持空间紧凑性。

Result: 在新视角合成（Novel View Synthesis）任务的标准benchmark上进行多场景验证。实验表明，在相同高斯预算下，LocusGS相比query-based Gaussian token基线方法提升了渲染质量；分析显示学习到的锚点形成连贯的空间布局，解码的高斯分布更结构化，散射token-associated primitives减少。

Conclusion: 1. 显式3D锚点状态有效解决了前馈3DGS管线中查询的空间 specialization 问题，使高斯预算与图像分辨率/输入视图数解耦的同时维持了空间结构。
2. 锚点细化机制依赖多视图几何一致性，对输入视图质量、相机 pose 精度及场景复杂度敏感；极端场景下锚点收敛可能存在歧义。
3. 该方法为查询式场景表示提供了显式几何 inductive bias，但锚点半径的动态范围策略和跨层细化机制的更深层理论分析仍有欠缺。

Abstract: Recent query-based feed-forward 3DGS methods represent a scene using learnable queries, each aggregating multi-view evidence and decoding a group of Gaussians. Ideally, different queries should specialize in coherent local regions of the scene. However, we observe that Gaussians decoded from the same query often scatter across distant scene regions, resulting in weak query-level spatial coherence and poor alignment with the scene structure. We attribute this behavior to the purely latent representation of existing Gaussian queries. To address this limitation, we introduce LocusGS, which augments each Gaussian query with a 3D anchor state consisting of a center and a support radius. The anchor state is progressively refined across decoder layers and is used throughout query interaction, multi-view feature aggregation, and Gaussian generation. Specifically, an anchor-to-ray geometric bias guides each query toward spatially relevant image observations, while anchor-centered decoding organizes its Gaussians within a local region. Experiments on novel view synthesis benchmarks show that LocusGS improves rendering quality over query-based Gaussian token baselines under the same Gaussian budget. Further analysis shows that the learned anchors form coherent spatial layouts and lead to more structured Gaussian distributions, demonstrating that explicit anchor states improve the spatial organization. Our project page: https://leo-frank.github.io/LocusGS_viewer.
