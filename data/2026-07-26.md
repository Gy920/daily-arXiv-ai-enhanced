### [1] [RealVDeblur: One-Step Diffusion for Generalizable Real-World Video Deblurring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20628)
*Renbiao Jin,Mingxin Yang,Yutian Chen,Junhao Zhuang,Xin Cai,Mulin Yu,Linning Xu,Wenxian Yu,Danping Zou,Shi Guo,Tianfan Xue*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: RealVDeblur 是一个基于单步扩散的视频去模糊框架，通过利用大规模 3DGS 合成数据与训练后端的窗口式掩码，实现对复杂真实世界视频去模糊的可泛化与高效推理。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 现有训练数据规模有限且物理仿真度低（如 GoPro 仅 20 个场景，合成常采用简单 RGB 平均），无法覆盖真实摄像运动、物体运动与离焦模糊的多样性。
2. 视频扩散模型依赖时序压缩 VAE，其平滑过渡假设与真实序列中帧间模糊差异大的特性冲突，且多步采样在长视频上计算昂贵并受位置编码外推不稳定影响。

Method: 1. 核心思路：将一个预训练的视频扩散模型重配为去模糊生成先验，通过无时序压缩的帧级编码注入模糊条件，并用单步蒸馏与训练无关的时序窗口掩码实现高效稳定推理。
2. 帧级编码：禁用 VAE 的时序压缩并采用逐帧 2D 编码，使潜在表征能保持帧级模糊差异，避免压缩后模糊信息被平滑。
3. 单步蒸馏：使用分布匹配蒸馏（DMD）将多步扩散采样压缩为一步生成器，大幅降低计算开销，更适合长视频部署。
4. 时序窗口掩码：在推理时将 3D RoPE 的有效范围限制在局部时间窗口，缓解长度外推不稳定，实现常数内存的无限长序列处理。

Result: 在多样化真实世界基准（包括不同设备、场景与严重运动模糊）上进行评估，报告了感知质量、语义保真度与时间一致性的优势；同时在严重模糊的 3D 重建下游任务中展示更好的鲁棒性；验证涵盖长视频且推理内存恒定。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：将视频生成先验引入去模糊并实现实际部署的完整路线——从大规模物理合成数据、架构适配到训练自由的长序列稳定推理，显著提升真实世界迁移能力。
2. 主要限制：数据合成依赖 3DGS 资产与高帧率视频，覆盖虽广但仍可能遗漏极端光学现象；且单步蒸馏可能损失部分生成多样性，对极严重模糊的恢复仍有限制。
3. 关键 takeaway：真实世界视频去模糊的突破不仅需要更真实数据，还需重新审视视频先验的时序建模与推理效率， curricula 的蒸馏与无训练窗口策略提供了可复用的设计模式。

Abstract: Real-world video deblurring remains challenging due to diverse motion patterns, complex degradations, and the scarcity of realistic training data, yet robust restoration is critical for downstream pipelines such as mobile imaging and 3D reconstruction. This work presents \textbf{RealVDeblur}, an efficient generative framework designed to improve in-the-wild robustness under diverse real capture conditions. First, a large-scale, physically grounded blur synthesis pipeline is constructed from scene-level 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) assets and high-frame-rate videos, providing realistic training data covering both camera-induced and object-motion blur. Second, a video diffusion prior is leveraged for restoration; to better accommodate frame-dependent blur variations, temporal compression in the VAE is disabled and a frame-wise encoding scheme is adopted. For practical deployment on long videos, multi-step diffusion sampling is distilled into an efficient one-step generator, and a training-free Temporal Window Mask stabilizes inference beyond the training horizon with constant memory usage. Extensive experiments on diverse real-world benchmarks demonstrate strong perceptual quality, semantic fidelity, and temporal consistency on unseen videos, as well as improved robustness in downstream 3D reconstruction under severe motion blur. Project page: https://rbjin.github.io/RealVDeblur

### [2] [3D-GIMP: When 3D Gaussian Inpainting Meets PatchMatch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20789)
*Xuening Tian,Dieter Schmalstieg,Shohei Mori*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出3D-GIMP混合范式：以单视图扩散修复为外观先验，通过3D感知PatchMatch实现跨视图纹理传播，解决3DGS场景物体移除中的多视图不一致与计算效率瓶颈，定位为一类替代纯迭代扩散的高效重建一致性方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 基于迭代扩散的多视图编辑计算昂贵，且难以生成锐利细节。
2. 扩散驱动的逐帧修复存在"hallucination drift"，导致多视图不一致及新视角结构伪影。
3. 纯生成式方法难以保证跨视图几何一致性，易产生模糊或扭曲重建。
4. 传统patch matching（如InpaintFusion）局限于已有视图内容，且依赖surfel表示，难与3DGS原生集成。

Method: 1. 核心思路：放弃逐视图扩散，采用"单视图生成先验+3D PatchMatch传播"的混合管线，以重建一致性优先取代迭代生成。
2. 在关键参考视图执行单次生成式inpainting建立外观先验，作为后续跨视图传播的纹理源。
3. 引入3D-aware PatchMatch算法，通过对应匹配将参考纹理传播至所有训练视图，消除逐帧扩散的随机性。
4. 提出Poisson梯度域深度完成方法，融合尺度模糊的单目深度先验与3DGS不完整场景深度，修复移除区域几何。

Result: 实验聚焦3DGS场景高保真物体移除任务，在保持与多视图扩散方法竞争性修复质量的同时，宣称在渲染速度与多视图一致性上取得显著优势。验证范围覆盖合成与真实场景重建流程，但文中未明确标注具体benchmark数据集名称，且未充分说明真实世界数据的评估细节。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：证明基于重建一致性优先的混合策略（单视图生成+匹配传播）可替代纯生成式迭代方法，在3DGS编辑中实现几何一致性与计算效率的更好权衡。
2. 主要限制：方法上限受限于初始参考视图修复质量与PatchMatch传播的几何精度；深度完成环节对单目先验的可靠性依赖较强，文中未充分说明其在复杂遮挡或无纹理区域的行为边界。
3. 关键Takeaway：避免3D场景编辑中不必要的扩散随机性，通过显式几何传播（而非隐式生成）维持多视图一致性，是该任务的高效路径，但泛化至无预重建3DGS场景的开放域编辑仍需进一步验证。

Abstract: Recent advances in 3D scene editing have leveraged iterative diffusion models to update input views. However, this process is computationally expensive and struggles to produce sharp details. Meanwhile, ``hallucination drift'' frequently introduces multi-view inconsistencies, leading to structural artifacts when rendering novel viewpoints. To address this problem, we present 3D-GIMP (3D Gaussian Inpainting Meets Patch Matching), a novel hybrid paradigm designed for high-fidelity object removal in 3D Gaussian Splatting. Instead of diffusing every view, 3D-GIMP performs a single generative inpainting on a key reference view, which serves as an appearance prior. We then introduce a 3D-aware PatchMatch algorithm to propagate these reference textures across all remaining views via correspondence matching, effectively bypassing the stochastic nature of frame-by-frame diffusion. By prioritizing reconstructive consistency over iterative generation, 3D-GIMP maintains high-frequency details across arbitrary resolutions while ensuring a mathematically consistent 3D reconstruction. Our experiments demonstrate that 3D-GIMP not only achieves competitive inpainting quality as previous methods using diffusion in multiple views, but also outperforms these methods in rendering speed and view consistency.

### [3] [SubSplat: High-Resolution Pixel-aligned 3DGS via Sub-pixel Gaussian Reparameterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20813)
*Jiun Lee,Jaekwang Kim,Sangmin Lee*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: SubSplat通过亚像素高斯重参数化在低分辨率特征输入下实现高分辨率渲染，解决了像素对齐高斯泼溅方法中计算成本与渲染质量之间的根本性权衡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 1. 像素对齐高斯泼溅方法的高分辨率渲染受限于计算瓶颈：提升输入分辨率以增加细节会导致骨干网络计算成本二次增长。
2. 低分辨率输入虽保持计算稳定，但导致高斯密度不足，产生模糊和光晕伪影，无法保留高频细节。
3. 现有方法将输出图元密度与骨干计算分辨率绑定，无法独立扩展渲染分辨率而不增加网络开销。

Method: 1. 核心思路：引入亚像素高斯重参数器(SPGR)在低分辨率特征基础上将初级高斯细分为细粒度图元，实现高分辨率渲染而无需提高骨干输入分辨率。
2. 设计SPGR模块，将网格锚定的每个初级高斯拆分为多个亚像素图元，通过专用的几何与外观特征实现内容感知的密度放大，并保持总不透明度守恒。
3. 集成可变形注意力进行跨视图特征聚合，将多视图信息融入高斯参数，使子像素图元的对齐更精确，提升高频细节和几何一致性。
4. 关键新意：首次将图元密度扩展与骨干网络计算解耦，使高分辨率输出不再依赖高分辨率特征网格。

Result: 在RealEstate10K和ACID数据集上进行新视点合成任务验证，覆盖多场景和不同目标分辨率，实验结果表明SubSplat在高分辨率渲染下显著降低延迟（约3倍以上），同时重建质量优于像素对齐基线及外部上采样方法。

Conclusion: 1. 核心价值：SPGR机制成功将高分辨率渲染的计算瓶颈从二次性转为与输入分辨率无关，实现了质量与效率的双赢。
2. 主要限制：子像素图元的生成质量高度依赖初级高斯的几何完整性与特征聚合效果，若低分辨率特征本身结构模糊，则再细分难以完全恢复细节。
3. 关键启示：高斯泼溅的渲染分辨率可通过重参数化独立扩展，为资源受限场景的高质量新视点合成提供了可扩展架构。

Abstract: Pixel-aligned Gaussian splatting enables efficient and generalizable novel-view synthesis. However, high-resolution rendering faces a critical trade-off where increasing input resolution improves detail at the expense of quadratically rising network computational cost. Conversely, maintaining low-resolution inputs stabilizes this cost but results in insufficient Gaussian density and artifacts. To address this, we propose SubSplat, which introduces Sub-pixel Gaussian Reparameterizer(SPGR) to subdivide primary Gaussians into fine-grained primitives, restoring structural density directly from low-resolution features. We further enhance the reparameterization quality through feature aggregation, which effectively captures high-frequency details across multiple views. Experiments on RealEstate10K and ACID demonstrate that SubSplat achieves high-fidelity rendering with superior efficiency. Our results validate that the proposed framework successfully resolves the trade-off between reparameterization fidelity and network computational cost inherent in pixel-aligned Gaussian Splatting.
